A quiet stop close to everything

Al-Sadiq Hotel stands on Railway Road in Sahiwal, opposite Model Bazar. Guests describe the staff as polite and helpful and the surroundings as calm and peaceful.

The hotel is close to the main road and has easy parking. Free breakfast and Wi-Fi come with your stay, and the front desk answers at any hour.
